# Prison Server Guide: How to Make the Most Out of It

This guide explains how we can maximize the potential of the new Prison Server (formerly Mining Server) for the best player experience and retention.

## 1. Core Gameplay Loop

The Prison server thrives on a satisfying, addictive grind. To make the most out of it, focus on:

- **Clear Progression:** Players should instantly understand that breaking blocks = money = better tools = new zones.
- **Satisfying Feedback:** Use sounds, particles, and action-bar messages when blocks are broken, tools level up, or zones are unlocked.
- **Milestones:** Place major upgrades just when the grind starts feeling repetitive. Introduce new mechanics (like enchants, auto-sell, backpacks) at these milestones.

## 2. Monetization & Economy

The Prison setup is highly suitable for monetization if done correctly without being pay-to-win:

- **Cosmetics:** Pickaxe skins, custom block-break particles, name tags, chat colors.
- **Boosters:** Global or personal multipliers for sell prices, experience, or block yields. Players love global boosters because they help the whole server.
- **Battle Pass:** Introduce a seasonal pass with daily and weekly challenges (e.g., "Break 5,000 diamond ore", "Reach Zone D").

## 3. Community & Competitive Elements

Although Mining/Prison can be solo-focused, adding community aspects increases retention:

- **Leaderboards (Holograms):** Blocks broken, money earned, highest prestige level.
- **Gangs/Clans:** Let players group up to combine their multipliers or share a private gang mine.
- **Events:** "Mine-Party" where a specific zone gives 2x money for 1 hour.

## 4. Expanding the Content (Endgame)

Once players reach the final zone, they need a reason to stay:

- **Prestige System:** Reset their rank/zones but grant a permanent multiplier and a chat prefix.
- **Custom Enchants:** Introduce highly expensive, endgame enchants (e.g., Explosive Pickaxe, Laser, Jackhammer).
- **PvP Mines:** High-risk, high-reward zones where players can fight for the best ores.

## 5. Technical Maintenance

To keep the server running smoothly:

- **Mine Resets:** Ensure mine resets are staggered and announced in chat so players aren't trapped or frustrated.
- **Anti-Cheat/Anti-Macro:** Since the core loop is mining, use strong anti-macro tools to ensure fair play.
- **Database Tuning:** The block-breaking rate will be very high. Ensure the database logging (if any) is batched and not writing on every block break to prevent lag.

By focusing on these areas, the Prison server will become a highly engaging, profitable, and smooth experience for the community.
